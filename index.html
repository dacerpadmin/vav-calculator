VAV Differential Pressure Calculator

Velocity pressure at a VAV inlet from airflow & inlet size. Air density fixed at 1.2 kg/m³.

Raw velocity pressure = k of 1. Set to your flow-ring's k (typ. 2–3) for the amplified sensor signal.
Inlet area — m²
Inlet velocity — m/s
Raw velocity pressure (Pv) — Pa
Amplified ΔP (Pv × k) — Pa

Formulas. Area A = π/4·d² (round) or A = W·H (square). Velocity v = Q/A with Q converted to m³/s (CFM ×0.00047195, L/s ×0.001, m³/h ÷3600). Velocity pressure Pv = ½·ρ·v², ρ = 1.2 kg/m³. Amplified ΔP = Pv·k.

Raw Pv is manufacturer-independent physics. The amplified ΔP is what an averaging flow ring reports; use the k value from your VAV box datasheet for an accurate sensor reading.
